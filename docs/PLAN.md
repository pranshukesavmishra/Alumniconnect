## The app in one line
A beautiful, trusted, free app where every JECian can build a profile, find and message anyone from JEC,
join their batch and interest groups, and bring their friends along.

## Key choices
- **UX target:** sign up in under 3 minutes and message a batchmate within 5. A design lead approves every UI change.
- **Stack:** React + Vite + TypeScript PWA; Android through a Trusted Web Activity on the Play Store; Supabase backend; Firebase push; Brevo email; Cloudflare Pages hosting.
- **Budget:** ₹0 a month, plus a one-time ₹2,100 Play Store fee (alumni sponsorship requested).
- **Timeline:** 14 weeks. Weeks 1–3 are design and user testing; public launch in week 14, batch by batch.

## Open decisions for the alumni committee
Section 23 of the PDF lists 8 decisions, each with the week it is needed by. The most urgent:
1. Endorsement and permission to use the JEC name and logo (week 2).
2. Access to college batch lists for verification (week 4).
3. Whether members may see the names of batchmates who haven't joined yet (week 9).
